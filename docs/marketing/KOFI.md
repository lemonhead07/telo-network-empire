## About

> **In 1983 you rent a room above a shop on Main Street, screw a sheet of plywood to the wall, and wire your first customer in by hand.**
>
> TELO is a first-person job sim and business game about running a phone company. You knock on doors, carry a ladder from the van, fit sockets, and plug people into a switchboard you can see from your bed. Every customer is a person with a name, a front door and opinions.
>
> Then the job changes. You hire Jory, who's right most of the time, and whose mistakes turn up weeks later. Work stops being a screwdriver and becomes a ticket, then a line on a terminal, then a lamp on a rack. By the end of early access you're bringing a whole small planet's calls into a data centre you built yourself, and the national phone monopoly, NezTel ("Nez Ensures Zero-downtime"), has started to notice.
>
> - **Do it by hand first:** wires, poles, junction boxes, a real switchboard. Bodge it if you're in a hurry; it'll come back to you.
> - **Then hand it over:** staff take tickets from you in person. They're quicker to hire than to train.
> - **One small planet:** walk it, drive the van round it in a quarter of an hour, take the ferry to the island.
> - **Every improvement creates a new problem.** That's the whole design, and most of the jokes.
>
> **Where it's at:** early access plays start to finish, Acts 0 to 5, 20 to 30 hours. Sound is being added now. The Steam page goes up in December, a free demo is in Steam Next Fest (22 Feb – 1 Mar 2027), and early access is planned for March 2027.
>
> **How it's made:** by one person who isn't a programmer, working with an AI assistant (Claude) that writes the code, the words and the scripts that build the models. Every decision about what goes in is a human one.
>
> Supporting here keeps it going. Thanks for picking up.

## Memberships

Three tiers. Each one is cheap to keep up: the monthly letter is drafted by Claude from the month's notes, and the builds come out of the release workflow.

### Subscriber — $3 a month
> You're on the line.
> - **A letter from the switchboard** once a month: what got built, a picture, what's next.
> - **Your name in the game's phone directory.** The credits are a 1983 phone book, and you're in it.

### Line crew — $7 a month
> Everything in Subscriber, plus:
> - **Test builds before Steam,** for Windows and Linux, about once a month. They're rough. Saves usually carry over; sometimes they don't.
> - **A vote** on what gets built next, from a short list.

### Exchange — $15 a month (limited to 25)
> Everything in Line crew, plus:
> - **Name a file on the Board.** The game has a bulletin board with a file area; one of the files is named by you (kept in the game's tone: no real brands, nothing rude).
> - **A Steam key** when early access launches, once you've been a member for three months.

*How to deliver test builds:* attach the zip to a members-only post if Ko-fi accepts a file that size (about 70 MB); otherwise put a link to it in a members-only post. Never post the link publicly.
