# The Ages: planet-scale gameplay, Acts 0–7

**Status:** 📄 design (idea session, 2026-11-15). Nothing past Act 1 is built.
**Why it exists:** so every system built now is shaped for the whole game. §6 is the list dev sessions should check before designing any new system: *"what later act also uses this?"*
**Reads with:** `EXPANSION_DESIGN.md` (Acts 0–1 in detail, as rounds 0–4), `TECH_TREE.md` (tasks and the hand-off ladder), `NEZ_AND_THE_SPINE.md` (actions, Nez, one plot line). Where this disagrees with those, they win until an idea session decides otherwise.
**Design canvas:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0a398f7c-ea51-4285-9488-2cba471067b4 (the "Ages" boards).

---

## 1. What never changes

Every age is the same game at a bigger size:

- **The five jobs:** Sell → Connect → Carry → Keep → Count → Hand over → go further out (`EXPANSION_DESIGN` §2).
- **The round recipe:** each round adds one new place, one new thing built by hand, one new bottleneck, one new hire and one new problem.
- **The seven verbs:** take, carry, fasten, connect, test, switch, say (`NEZ_AND_THE_SPINE` §4). Every job below is written in these verbs. **No age needs a new verb**: a rocket stage is fastened, a satellite is switched on, an auction is a "say". New ages bring new *assemblies* and *places*, not new controls.
- **Actions anyone can do:** every job is an action the player, staff, Nez or a test can perform.
- **The hand-off ladder:** do it → show it → hand it off → automate it → maintain it. The player is always fastest.
- **Every improvement creates a new problem.**

## 2. The planet, age by age

The planet stays 2.5 km radius (D12). What changes is how much of it you can reach and what you put there.

| Act | Region opens | Places | What's in the way |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Home Bay: the town | Main Street, Hollow Farm | Nothing; Distance |
| 1 | Home Bay: the coast, ridge and island | Saltmarsh Quay, Harrow Ridge, Gull Rock, Easthaven | River, one shared cable; A cliff; The sea; All of it |
| 2 | The plains inland | Millbrook, Northfield College, Copperhill Quarry | Nez owns the only trunk; Students want more than you have; Blasting shakes every joint |
| 3 | The ocean and Farland's near coast | Easthaven Docks, Kestrel Point, Farland | Power and heat; Nez's landing permit; The ocean |
| 4 | Suburbs and the Interior | Larkfield Estate, Crown Heights and Tannery Row, The Interior | No ducts; Money; Distance, cost |
| 5 | The Teeth, the highway, the seas | The Teeth, The Ring Highway, Meridian, The fishing fleet | Height, weather; People moving; The auction house; No ground |
| 6 | Glasspan and Nez's heartland | Glasspan, Every town's edge, Nadir | Heat; unlimited sun; Space and power; Nez |
| 7 | The equator, the pole, orbit | Cape Lantern, Rime Station, Orbit, Exchange 0 | Gravity; Cold, solar storms; Passes overhead; — |

**Geography rules that carry the whole game:** wire has a length limit; radio needs to see over hills and the curve of the planet; water stops poles; height costs fuel and weather; the antipode is half a lap away, and it belongs to Nez. A new place is worth building only if it has **one thing in the way that teaches one new build** (`EXPANSION_DESIGN` §5).

New names introduced here (all fictional): **Millbrook**, **Northfield College**, **Copperhill Quarry**, **Easthaven Docks**, **Kestrel Point**, **Farland** (the far continent), **Larkfield Estate**, **Crown Heights**, **Tannery Row**, **The Interior**, **The Teeth** (mountain spine), **The Ring Highway**, **Meridian** (capital, on Farland), **Glasspan** (desert basin), **Nadir** (Nez's city at the antipode), **Cape Lantern** (launch site), **Rime Station** (pole).

## 5. Keeping your hands in the game at planet scale

The fear with a game that ends in orbit: by Act 5 you're only reading dashboards. Five rules stop that:

1. **Flagship jobs.** Every age has a few jobs only you can do, listed above: the first dish on Harrow Ridge, the last splice on the beach, aiming the first tower on the Teeth, the first launch. They are the big moments of each age, done first-person with the same verbs as the first install.
2. **Take over, at any size.** Fly to any site and take over any job from any crew: you finish fastest and they learn (`TECH_TREE` §4). In later acts it also raises morale in that region.
3. **Nez's par time.** Every job carries the time Nez's by-the-book crews take. Beating it by hand is its own small win, at every scale.
4. **Hand-offs get bigger, not fewer.** Act 0 hands over one job; Act 2 a depot; Act 4 a region; Act 7 the whole planet to machines. Each is still a person or a thing you choose, with a flaw.
5. **The Blackout is a manual finale.** In 2034 every automated stage drops to rung one. What saves the network is what you kept: your own mastery, the Procedures Binder, and the oldest staff who still remember the switchboard. Act 7's crisis is played with Act 0's skills.

### Six things to bake in while doing spine step 1 and TT1

1. **Action scale:** task, job or project in one shape (minutes to weeks).
2. **An owner on every asset** (you, Nez, a rival, a co-op).
3. **An open-ended link medium** (copper to satellite).
4. **A region id** on every site, asset, staff member and subscriber.
5. **Vehicles and travel as data**, read from the planet.
6. **Money as whole cents in `long`**, counts as `long`: Act 6 has ten million things.

### Not to build early

Orbit, auctions, the regulator, finance and cascades are designed here only so their *shape* is known. None of them should be built before its act is next, and each arrives as data plus one rule wherever possible.

## 7. Verb check


128 jobs across eight acts, all written in the seven verbs: **take** 6, **carry** 26, **fasten** 30, **connect** 26, **test** 36, **switch** 30, **say** 55. "Say" carries most of the late game (auctions, contracts, inquiries), so a richer *say* (choices with consequences, people who remember) is worth investing in early; it's what doors and Jory already use.

## 8. Open questions (for an idea session)

- **A1. Where is Exchange 0?** Options: under Nez's exchange in Nadir; your first room on Main Street; somewhere you haven't built yet. Ties to N2 (is Nez who you become?).
- **A2. Does Nez fall, merge or become you?** Act 6 offers a merger; Act 7 settles it.
- **A3. How much of Farland is playable at 1.0?** D8 says 1.0 reaches undersea cables to other continents (Act 3–4). Suggest: Farland's near coast only, Nadir from Act 6.
- **A4. Do towns grow visibly** (new houses appear on the planet) or only as numbers? Visible is better and costs art.
- **A5. Is the launch first-person?** Suggest yes: you fasten the last stage and throw the switch, then watch from the beach.
- **A6. Day length late in the game.** With a year-long planner, a day is mostly skipped. Fine, or should late acts change what a "day" is (a week per sleep)?
